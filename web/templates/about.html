ABOUT FLORACORE

Growing plants shouldn’t have to be guesswork.

FloraCore began with a simple idea: what if plant care could be more intelligent, more reliable, and easier to understand?

01 / WHY

Plants are always telling us something.

Plants constantly respond to their environment—soil moisture, light, water, time, temperature, and countless small changes. Yet most of us still care for them by guessing when something looks wrong.

FloraCore is our attempt to change that. We’re building a connected plant-care platform that combines sensors, automation, embedded hardware, and software into one system designed to understand what is happening around a plant and help people respond to it.

02 / SYSTEM

Hardware meets FloraOS.

FloraCore is the physical system interacting with the plant and its environment. FloraOS is the software layer that turns those measurements and devices into monitoring, automation, insights, and integrations.

PLANT↓ SENSORS↓ FLORACORE↓ FLORAOS

The goal is not simply to create a flower pot filled with electronics. The goal is to build a platform for connected plant care.

03 / PHILOSOPHY

Automation without losing control.

We believe good automation should remove repetitive work without removing the human from the process.

FloraCore should handle the things machines are good at—monitoring continuously, reacting consistently, keeping records, and noticing patterns—while leaving meaningful decisions in the hands of the person growing the plant.

Instead of hiding everything behind a mysterious algorithm, FloraOS should make information understandable. You should be able to see what your plant is experiencing, what FloraCore is doing, and why.

04 / FOUNDATION

Built as a real system.

FloraCore is designed as more than a standalone prototype. The hardware, FloraOS backend, account system, secure device communication, telemetry storage, and developer API are being developed as parts of one connected architecture.

The physical FloraCore authenticates itself securely to FloraOS, while users and applications interact through a separate account and API layer. That separation lets the platform grow without sacrificing the security boundary around the physical device.

05 / OPEN DEVELOPMENT

Designed to be open.

We don’t want FloraCore to become a closed box that only works with one website. FloraOS is being designed so developers, researchers, makers, and users can understand how it works, build on top of it, and create their own integrations.

Through the FloraOS Developer API, authorized applications can access device information, telemetry, plant profiles, and other account-owned resources without exposing the private credentials used to authenticate the physical FloraCore itself.

As the project matures, we plan to publicly release FloraCore and FloraOS as open-source software under the GNU AGPL/GPL family of licenses.

We want people to be able to study the system, modify it, improve it, self-host parts of it, and build new ideas on top of what we create. The copyleft principles of the AGPL and GPL can help keep improvements to the open project available to the wider community.

Our intention is for FloraCore to grow not only as a product, but as an open platform that other people can learn from and contribute to.

OUR PRINCIPLES

What we build around.

01

Useful automation.

Technology should solve an actual problem instead of existing simply because it can.

02

Understandable data.

Measurements should help people understand their plants rather than overwhelm them with numbers.

03

Security by separation.

Hardware identity, user identity, ownership, and application access should remain distinct.

04

Room to grow.

FloraCore should evolve as new sensors, software capabilities, integrations, and ideas emerge.

06 / WHAT COMES NEXT

We’re still building.

What exists today is only the foundation. Our long-term vision is to create a secure, open platform for connected plant care—one that can be used, studied, modified, extended, and improved by the people who build with it.

Better sensing. Better automation. Better software. Open development. And, hopefully, healthier plants.
This is FloraCore. Technology built around something alive — and built to be shared.
